# Objective

The objective of this task was to work on the development of a personal AI-based project named **Dr Drive**, designed to assist users in evaluating the health and condition of used vehicles. The project aims to combine vehicle diagnostic data, computer vision, and machine learning techniques to detect faults, predict failures, estimate repair costs, and determine the fair resale value of a vehicle.

---

# Introduction

Purchasing a used vehicle often involves uncertainty regarding the actual condition of the car. Many mechanical issues remain hidden until after purchase, leading to unexpected maintenance expenses. To address this problem, an intelligent vehicle inspection system was conceptualized and developed.

**Dr Drive** is an AI-powered Android application that analyzes vehicle health using OBD-II diagnostic data and computer vision-based damage detection. The system provides users with detailed insights regarding vehicle condition, potential failures, repair costs, fraud indicators, and resale valuation.

The project integrates mobile development, backend engineering, machine learning, and computer vision technologies into a single intelligent platform.

# Key Features

## 1. OBD-II Bluetooth Integration

The system connects with ELM327-compatible OBD-II devices and retrieves real-time vehicle diagnostic information.

Data Collected:

- Engine RPM
- Vehicle Speed
- Engine Temperature
- Fuel Consumption
- Battery Voltage
- Trouble Codes (DTCs)

### Benefits

- Real-time vehicle monitoring
- Fault diagnosis
- Health analysis

---

## 2. Image-Based Damage Detection

Computer Vision techniques are used to analyze vehicle images and detect visible damages.

Technology Used:

- YOLOv8
- OpenCV

Detected Issues:

- Dents
- Scratches
- Broken Parts
- Exterior Damage

### Benefits

- Automated visual inspection
- Damage severity assessment
- Faster vehicle evaluation

---

## 3. AI-Based Health Scoring

Machine learning models analyze vehicle parameters and generate an overall health score.

### Output

- Excellent
- Good
- Average
- Poor

### Benefits

- Easy understanding of vehicle condition
- Quick decision-making

---

## 4. Failure Prediction

Predictive machine learning models estimate the probability of future component failures.

Models Used:

- XGBoost
- Random Forest

Predicted Components:

- Battery
- Engine
- Cooling System
- Braking System

### Benefits

- Preventive maintenance
- Reduced repair costs

---

## 5. Repair Cost Estimation

The system estimates repair expenses based on identified faults and damage severity.

Features:

- India-specific cost estimation
- Parts and labor consideration
- Repair recommendations

### Benefits

- Transparent maintenance costs
- Better budgeting

---

## 6. Car Value Calculator

The platform estimates the fair resale value of a vehicle based on:

- Vehicle Age
- Mileage
- Brand
- Vehicle Condition
- Damage Assessment
- Market Trends

### Benefits

- Fair pricing
- Better negotiation support

---

## 7. Fraud Detection

The application identifies suspicious indicators that may suggest vehicle fraud.

Examples:

- Odometer Manipulation
- Hidden Damage
- Inconsistent Vehicle Health Data

### Benefits

- Increased buyer confidence
- Reduced fraud risk
